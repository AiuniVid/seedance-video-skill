# Seedance prompt templates

Fill-in templates for the jobs people most often bring to Seedance. Replace everything in `{braces}`.
They follow the BytePlus Dreamina Seedance 2.5 prompt guide and also run on Seedance 2.0.
More examples with settings: https://aiunivid.com/en/blog/seedance-prompt-examples

## Vertical story shot with a recurring character

```
Vertical 9:16, {10}-second shot, {stylized 3D animation}.
@image1 is the main character only. Keep face, hair and outfit identical.
@image2 is the product only. Keep its shape, colour and logo unchanged.
Scene: {a small apartment hallway, evening light}.
Action: {the courier} {hands over the parcel}, then {steps back, surprised}.
Camera: slow push-in on {the courier's face}, then a quick whip-pan to {the parcel}.
Dialogue: {NEIGHBOUR}: "{You're not the usual driver.}" Only {NEIGHBOUR} speaks.
No subtitles, no on-screen text.
```

## Opening hook

```
Vertical 9:16, {5}-second shot, {cinematic live-action look}.
Start in the middle of the moment, no establishing shot.
{A young chef} {drops a plate on the kitchen floor} during {opening night}.
{The head chef} {turns around slowly}.
Camera: handheld, fast push-in on {the young chef's face} in the last second.
{HEAD CHEF}: "{Again.}"
```

## Motion-reference demo (exercise, dance, drill)

```
{16:9}, {5}-second single continuous take, real-time speed, no cuts.
@image1 is the only person in the video. Keep identity, outfit and proportions.
@video1 is a motion reference only. Copy the movement, timing and footwork,
not the person or the background.
Action: exactly one {kettlebell swing}, from {a hinged start position} to {a standing lockout}.
Camera: locked, eye level, {three-quarter front} view, full body in frame.
Setting: {plain studio floor}, even light.
```

## Storyboard to a cinematic shot

```
{16:9}, {10}-second cinematic shot, one continuous take.
@image1 is the storyboard. Use it for plot, composition and scene order only.
@image2 is {the knight}: keep face, armour and proportions.
@image3 is {the frozen bridge}: keep layout, light and palette.
Scene: {the knight crosses the bridge at dawn as the ice starts to crack}.
Camera: {slow tracking shot from behind, then a low-angle reveal of the far gate}.
Style: {stylized 3D game cinematic}, {cold blue light, drifting snow}.
Generate one continuous scene, not a collage of panels.
```

## Product clip from one still (image-to-video)

```
Use the uploaded image as the first frame.
Keep the {ceramic mug} unchanged: shape, colour, material, logo and printed text.
Motion: {a slow half turn on the counter while morning light moves across the glaze}.
Camera: {static, slight push-in}.
Background: {keep the kitchen counter; add soft steam rising from the mug}.
No new text, no extra products, no hands.
```

## Next shot, continuing from the last frame (image-to-video)

```
Use the uploaded image as the first frame; it is the last frame of the previous shot.
Keep {the character}'s face, outfit and the lighting unchanged.
Action: {she turns toward the door and opens it}.
Camera: {follow from behind at shoulder height}.
{MAYA}: "{Who's there?}" Only {MAYA} speaks.
```

## Character sheet (generate_image, before the video)

```
Character turnaround sheet of {a cheerful 3D-animated courier in a yellow jacket},
front, three-quarter and side views on a plain light-grey background,
consistent face, hair and outfit, full body, soft studio light, no text.
```
